Straight answers

The questions everyone asks.

What happens if I stop paying?

Eventually, but never mid month and never without warning. You always keep the full month you paid for, and if a payment fails I email you and wait at least seven days. After that, if the subscription has ended, the site goes offline. I say all of this up front because finding out at month eight is how people get burned.

Who owns the domain?

You do, always. You buy it, it stays in your name, and I never hold it. If the site ever comes down, your domain is untouched and you can point it anywhere the same day.

How fast is the build?

About two days once I have your content. Review usually adds a few more. Nothing is due until you have seen the finished site.

Are you an agency?

No. It is me. I write the HTML, CSS and JavaScript myself, and I am the person who answers when you email about changing your hours.

Why is it this cheap?

I am a senior at Miami University and this is not my rent. The tradeoff is that I take on a small number of builds at a time and I am slower during exam weeks.

What is not included?

Online ordering, booking systems, payments, logins, logo design, photography, ad campaigns and SEO retainers. Any of it can be quoted separately. None of it is a hidden fee.
