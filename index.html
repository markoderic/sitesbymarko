The agreement, in plain words

  1. I build your website and I keep it updated. You pay $100 a month, or $1,000 once if you would rather own it outright.
  2. On the monthly plan the site lives on my hosting. You are subscribing to a working website, the same way you subscribe to Squarespace. You are not buying one.
  3. You always get the full month you have paid for. If you cancel halfway through June, your site stays up until the end of June. I never take back a month you have already paid.
  4. If a payment fails I email you and wait at least seven days before anything happens. Nothing ever goes offline without a warning first.
  5. After that, if the subscription has ended, the site comes offline. There is no contract and no cancellation fee, because the hosting is the only thing holding it up.
  6. Your domain name is yours. You buy it, it stays in your name, and I never hold it. If the site ever comes down your domain is untouched and you can point it anywhere.
  7. Nothing is due until you have seen the finished site and told me it is right.
